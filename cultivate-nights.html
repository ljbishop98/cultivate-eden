Cultivate Nights

Adam's task in the garden was to cultivate and keep a place of communion with God. Cultivate Nights are our way of practicing that same task — together, as a community, on purpose.

What to Expect

An evening set apart for communion

Every Cultivate Night is shaped around the same invitation Eden extended to Adam and Eve: come near, be known, and dwell in fellowship with the Father. We gather to worship, to open Scripture together, to pray for one another, and to practice the kind of honest fellowship that sin once broke and Christ has restored.

Worship

Unhurried time to encounter God's presence together, not just sing about Him.

The Word

Teaching that traces the story of Scripture — creation, fall, redemption, restoration.

Prayer & Fellowship

Space to be known and to carry one another, the way community was always meant to work.
