The Message

Not a garden. A meeting place.

In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth, and on the sixth day, He made mankind in His own image and likeness. Then He planted a garden — Eden, meaning pleasure — and placed man there to cultivate and keep it.

To Adam and Eve, this was more than soil and trees. It was where God was not known merely as Creator, but as Father. A place of harmony, intimacy, and fellowship, where sin had no foothold and fear had no name. Adam's task was never only agricultural — he was tending a place where humanity had communion with its Maker.

Fellowship. Communion. Peace with God. Delight. This is what Eden represented — and it's what God is calling us back to.

We know how the story continues. The serpent deceived Adam and Eve, and disobedience severed that communion. But separation was never God's final word. In mercy, He set a plan of redemption, reconciliation, and restoration in motion — to bring us back to the place Adam and Eve knew before the fall.

God has reconciled us and brought us back into fellowship with Him. Now He calls us to steward that place of intimacy — cultivating it, keeping it, and inviting others to be reconciled to Eden.
