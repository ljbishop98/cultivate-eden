A Friday Night Series

The Shabbat Series

"And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work." — Genesis 2:2

Rest was never an afterthought — it was part of God's original design, woven into creation itself alongside the garden He gave us to cultivate. For 8-10 weeks, we're gathering each Friday night to slow down, unlearn the hurry, and practice Sabbath together.

Why Sabbath

Rest was part of the design

Before sin ever entered the story, God had already built rhythm into creation: six days of work, and a seventh set apart to rest, delight, and be present with Him. Sabbath wasn't a rule added later — it was woven into Eden from the start, the same way communion was.

Just as Adam was given the garden to cultivate, we've been given rhythms of rest to steward. The Shabbat Series is our way of reclaiming that rhythm — not as a legal requirement, but as an invitation back to the pace God designed us for.

Sabbath isn't the absence of work. It's the presence of God, unhurried.
